# Brand and Content Licensing

This document separates **software code** from **institutional and educational assets**.

## 1. Software code

At present, the BITIRO Lab source code is **not open source** and is covered by the repository's
rights-reserved `LICENSE` file. A future source-code license may be adopted only after the
necessary ownership and authorization questions are resolved in writing.

## 2. Fundación Gabriel & Mary Mustakis

The following are excluded from any software license unless Fundación Mustakis expressly
authorizes otherwise in writing:

- the name “Fundación Gabriel & Mary Mustakis”;
- logos, wordmarks, symbols and visual identity;
- photographs and audiovisual assets;
- institutional copy and promotional materials;
- workshop, session and challenge content supplied by the Foundation;
- other institutional resources.

Files located under paths such as `public/brand/mustakis/` should be treated as institutional
assets and not as open-source software.

## 3. Partner institutions

Names, logos and visual resources of participating universities and partner institutions remain
the property of their respective owners and are not licensed by BITIRO Lab.

## 4. IROH / BITIRO artwork

Original BITIRO Lab interface artwork and generated mascot artwork should not be assumed to be
openly reusable merely because it is stored in this repository. It remains subject to the
repository's rights-reserved status and, where it incorporates institutional branding, any
additional permission requirements applicable to that branding.

## 5. Educational content

Educational content, session structure, guides, challenges and other learning materials must be
reviewed individually before publication or redistribution. Public availability of a program or
methodology does not, by itself, mean that every associated document, image, logo or software
asset is in the public domain or under an open-source license.

## 6. Future structure

If written authorization is obtained, a future release may separate licensing as follows:

- source code: a specifically stated software license;
- institutional branding/content: rights reserved / permission required;
- third-party assets: governed by their respective licenses or permissions.
